Who we are

About Missionary Ventures Australia

Missionary Ventures Australia (MVA) is an interdenominational Christian organisation and part of Missionary Ventures International based in Orlando, Florida, USA.

We are a family of Christians from around the globe bound together by our love for God and for others. We all share the common goal of reaching the world with the love of Jesus Christ and the Gospel of the Kingdom.

MVA seeks to promote and establish effective interdependent partnerships so we can help equip and support indigenous Churches and Christian organisations within the Asia/Pacific region helping them to fulfill their God given vision and mandates.

  • Church strengthening

    Supporting churches focused on gospel proclamation and disciple-making.

  • Discipleship

    Multiplying disciples through mentoring, Bible study, and ministry that fits the place.

  • Compassion

    Showing the love of Christ by meeting practical needs and by relief when crisis hits.

Our mission

Core mission

Equipping people to impact others for Christ

The development of sustainable partnerships that will help mobilize Christian communities to make disciples who will see lives transformed across the Asia/Pacific region.

Serving the Church

We exist to serve the Church and to fulfil the Great Commission of making disciples of all nations.

Motivated by love

Everything we do is motivated by the love of Christ, reaching the world with compassion and grace.

Absolute accountability

We maintain absolute accountability in all areas of our ministry, finances, and relationships.

God's Kingdom first

We prioritise God's Kingdom above all else, seeking his will in every decision and direction.

Raising up leaders

We are committed to raising up national Christian leaders who will serve their own communities.

Collaborative

Cross-cultural partnerships with ministry leaders, and room for more than one way of doing the work.

What we do

The work

Six strands, all of them with national churches and Christian organisations.

  1. 01

    Supporting church planters

    Walking with church planters in the Asia-Pacific: resources, training, and steady encouragement for local congregations.

  2. 02

    Partnerships

    Partnerships with churches and Christian organisations, so the Gospel can be shared more widely across the region.

  3. 03

    Short-term teams

    Short-term mission teams that meet training needs, bring resources, and help when there is an emergency.

  4. 04

    Emergency relief

    When disaster hits, practical aid and longer support, given in the name of Jesus.

  5. 05

    Project funds

    Money for projects local churches have asked for, including buildings and other practical needs.

  6. 06

    Schools

    Christ-centred schools where discipleship and study sit together.

Get involved

Mission team trips

MVA invites churches, schools, youth groups, and individuals to take part in trips across the Asia Pacific, with national leaders in the Philippines, East Malaysia, Indonesia, Cambodia, Thailand, Fiji, and Vanuatu.

Duration
10 to 14 days, and up to 3 months when a longer stay is right
Team size
About 5 to 12 people
Planning
Begin at least 6 months before departure
Who can join
Youth through to older adults

Preparation covers

  • Managing expectations
  • Spiritual discernment
  • Team unity
  • Conflict resolution
  • Cross-cultural preparation
  • Spiritual warfare
