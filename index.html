Who We Are

About Missionary Ventures Australia

Missionary Ventures Australia (MVA) is an interdenominational Christian organisation and part of Missionary Ventures International based in Orlando, Florida, USA.

We are a family of Christians from around the globe bound together by our love for God and for others. We all share the common goal of reaching the world with the love of Jesus Christ and the Gospel of the Kingdom.

MVA seeks to promote and establish effective interdependent partnerships so we can help equip and support indigenous Churches and Christian organisations within the Asia/Pacific region helping them to fulfill their God given vision and mandates.

Church Strengthening

Supporting churches focused on gospel proclamation and disciple-making

Discipleship

Multiplying disciples through mentoring, Bible study, and contextual ministry

Compassion

Demonstrating Christ's love by addressing community needs and providing relief

Our Mission
Our Core Mission

Equipping People to Impact Others for Christ

The development of sustainable partnerships that will help mobilize Christian communities to make disciples who will see lives transformed across the Asia/Pacific region.

Serving the Church

We exist to serve the Church and to fulfil the Great Commission of making disciples of all nations.

Motivated by Love

Everything we do is motivated by the love of Christ, reaching the world with compassion and grace.

Absolute Accountability

We maintain absolute accountability in all areas of our ministry, finances, and relationships.

God's Kingdom First

We prioritize God's Kingdom above all else, seeking His will in every decision and direction.

Raising Up Leaders

We are committed to raising up national Christian leaders who will serve their own communities.

Collaborative

Cross-cultural partnerships with ministry leaders, celebrating diverse ministry expressions.

What We Do

What the work looks like

01

Supporting Church Planters

We come alongside church planters throughout Asia-Pacific, providing resources, training, and ongoing encouragement to strengthen local congregations.

02

Strategic Partnerships

Partnerships with churches and Christian organisations, so the Gospel can be shared more widely across the region.

03

Short-Term Mission Teams

Sending dynamic short-term mission teams that address training needs, provide resources, and support emergency relief efforts.

04

Emergency Relief

Responding to natural disasters and crises, providing immediate aid and long-term support to affected communities in Jesus' name.

05

Fundraising for Projects

Raising funds for projects local churches have asked for, including buildings and other practical needs.

06

School Strengthening

Advancing Christ-centered educational institutions that integrate discipleship with academic excellence across the region.

Mission Team Trips

MVA invites churches, schools, youth groups, and individuals to participate in mission trips across the Asia Pacific region, partnering with national leaders in the Philippines, East Malaysia, Indonesia, Cambodia, Thailand, Fiji, and Vanuatu.

Duration 10-14 days (flexible, up to 3 months)
Team Size Approximately 5-12 people
Planning Timeline Begin at least 6 months before departure
Who Can Join Youth to older adults welcome

Team Training Covers:

Managing Expectations Spiritual Discernment Team Unity Conflict Resolution Cross-Cultural Prep Spiritual Warfare
