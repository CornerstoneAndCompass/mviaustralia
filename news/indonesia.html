MV Indonesia

August 27, 2025
Ledianus Gulu, Director of MV Indonesia

The work of Missionary Ventures in Indonesia continues to grow and bear fruit across the nation. Under the leadership of Director Ledianus Gulu, MV Indonesia has been establishing new partnerships with local churches and communities, creating lasting impact in some of the most remote areas of the archipelago.

Our teams have been focused on church planting, leadership development, and community outreach programs that address both spiritual and practical needs. The response from local communities has been overwhelmingly positive, with many coming to faith and existing believers being strengthened in their walk.

We are grateful for the continued support from MVA and partners around the world who make this vital work possible. Your prayers and financial support directly enable us to reach more communities with the hope of the Gospel.
