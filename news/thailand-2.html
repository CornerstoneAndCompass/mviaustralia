Thailand Mission Reflections: Part 2 - Eyes Opened, Lives Changed

March 13, 2025
Andrew George, CMA Alabama Area Rep

As I sit at my desk considering how to summarize a life changing trip, the memories flood back with vivid clarity. Our team arrived in Thailand with open hearts and willing hands, not fully knowing what God had in store for us.

What we witnessed over those two weeks transformed our understanding of mission work. The national leaders we partnered with showed us what it means to serve with joy in the most challenging circumstances. Their faith was infectious, their hospitality overwhelming.

We worked alongside local churches, helped with community projects, and shared in the daily rhythms of Thai Christian life. The experience opened our eyes to the incredible work God is doing in Southeast Asia and changed us forever.
