Thailand mission reflections, part 2: eyes opened, lives changed

Andrew George, CMA Alabama area representative

I have been trying to set down what those days in Thailand were like, and the memories are still clear. Our team arrived willing to work, without a full picture of what the fortnight would hold.

The national leaders we partnered with served with joy in hard circumstances. They were glad to have us, and they looked after us. We worked with local churches, helped with community projects, and shared the ordinary days of Thai Christian life.

The fortnight showed us the work God is doing in Southeast Asia, and it changed how we see that work.
