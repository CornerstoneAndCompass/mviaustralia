Mission Reflections: Part 1 - Seeing with the Heart

March 9, 2025
Mary Gilbert, Thailand Team

I am Mary Gilbert and part of the Thailand team. My husband, Rex, was also on this trip. From the moment we stepped off the plane, we knew God had something particular in mind for these days.

Seeing with the heart means looking beyond the surface, beyond the language barriers and cultural differences, to see people the way God sees them. In every village we visited, in every church gathering, we saw the fingerprints of God at work.

The Thai people welcomed us with open arms and shared their stories of faith, perseverance, and hope. Their testimonies reminded us that the Gospel truly is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes, regardless of geography or culture.
