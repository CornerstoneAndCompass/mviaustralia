Team Trips

Join a mission trip across the Asia Pacific region

Mission Trip Opportunities

MVA invites churches, schools, youth groups, and individuals to participate in mission trips across the Asia Pacific region, partnering with national leaders in the Philippines, East Malaysia, Indonesia, Cambodia, Thailand, Fiji, and Vanuatu.

Duration

10-14 days flexible up to 3 months

Team Size

5-12 people

Planning Timeline

Begin at least 6 months before

Who Can Join

Youth to older adults welcome

Team Training

Before a team travels, the preparation covers:

Managing Expectations Spiritual Discernment Personal Identity Team Unity Conflict Resolution Spiritual Warfare Cross-Cultural Preparation

What's Included

  • Airfares
  • In-country transport
  • Food and accommodation
  • Visas
  • Travel insurance
  • Comprehensive team training

MVA provides experienced team leaders or can train your preferred leaders. Teams from schools, churches, youth groups, or community organisations are all welcome.

Ask about a team trip

Write if a church, school, youth group, or a few friends want to travel with national leaders.
