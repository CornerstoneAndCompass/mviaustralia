Team trips

Churches, schools, youth groups, and individuals, travelling with national leaders.

Where teams go

MVA invites churches, schools, youth groups, and individuals to take part in trips across the Asia Pacific, with national leaders in the Philippines, East Malaysia, Indonesia, Cambodia, Thailand, Fiji, and Vanuatu.

Duration
10 to 14 days, and up to 3 months
Team size
5 to 12 people
Planning
Begin at least 6 months before
Who can join
Youth through to older adults

Before you travel

Preparation with the team covers:

  • Managing expectations
  • Spiritual discernment
  • Personal identity
  • Team unity
  • Conflict resolution
  • Spiritual warfare
  • Cross-cultural preparation

What the cost covers

  • Airfares
  • In-country transport
  • Food and accommodation
  • Visas
  • Travel insurance
  • Team preparation

MVA provides experienced team leaders, or can train leaders you already trust. Teams from schools, churches, youth groups, or community organisations are welcome.

Ask about a team

Write to the office if a church, a school, a youth group, or a few friends want to travel. Teams partner with local leaders.
