Our Impact

A humble account of the work this office shares, and an invitation to read the stories behind it.

AUSTRALIA

What this office is part of

Missionary Ventures Australia walks with national leaders in nine Asia-Pacific countries. The wider family has been at this since 1983. These are markers of the work, not a scoreboard.

01

Nine countries

Cambodia, Fiji, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand, Timor Leste, and Vanuatu, each with its own partners.

02

With the local church

The work sits with national leaders. Teams and gifts from Australia come alongside what those churches are already doing.

03

Practical care

When flood, earthquake, or another crisis hits a partner community, this office helps with relief that has been asked for.

04

Churches and schools

Support for church planters, and for Christ-centred schools where discipleship and study sit together.
