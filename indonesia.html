MV Indonesia

Ledianus Gulu, director of MV Indonesia

The work of Missionary Ventures in Indonesia continues under director Ledianus Gulu. MV Indonesia has been forming partnerships with local churches and communities, including in remote parts of the archipelago.

The teams are taken up with church planting, leadership development, and community outreach that meets both spiritual and practical needs. People have come to faith, and believers already in the churches have been strengthened.

Support from MVA and from partners elsewhere is what makes the visits possible. Prayer and financial gifts are used to reach further communities with the Gospel.
