Our purpose

Equipping people to impact others for Christ across the Asia-Pacific.

Core mission

Equipping people to impact others for Christ and the development of sustainable partnerships that will help mobilize Christian communities to make disciples who will see lives transformed across the Asia/Pacific region.

The partnerships, the team trips, and the work of raising up leaders all sit under that sentence.

What guides us

Five commitments. They are also written out on the home page, with a sixth note on working side by side.

Serving the Church

We exist to serve the Church and to fulfil the Great Commission of making disciples of all nations.

Motivated by love

Everything we do is motivated by the love of Christ, reaching the world with compassion and grace.

Absolute accountability

We maintain absolute accountability in all areas of our ministry, finances, and relationships.

God's Kingdom first

We prioritise God's Kingdom above all else, seeking his will in every decision and direction.

Raising up leaders

We are committed to raising up national Christian leaders who will serve their own communities.

How we go about it

MVA partners with national leaders who have strong relationships with churches and Christian organisations. The partnerships are cross-cultural, and the shape of ministry is not the same in every place. The aim is to walk with local communities, not to build a dependency on teams from outside.

  1. 01

    Alongside

    We work with local leaders, and we respect their vision and their authority in their own communities.

  2. 02

    Cross-cultural

    We honour the culture we are guests in, including the way ministry is expressed there.

  3. 03

    After the team goes home

    We want the relationships, and the work, to continue after a team has gone home.

Be part of the mission
