MISSION AND BELIEFS

Mission and Beliefs

Equipping people to impact others for Christ across the Asia/Pacific region

CORE MISSION

Equipping people to impact others for Christ and the development of sustainable partnerships that will help mobilize Christian communities to make disciples who will see lives transformed across the Asia/Pacific region.

The partnerships, the team trips, and the work of raising up leaders all sit under that sentence.

CORE VALUES

What we hold

Five commitments we keep coming back to.

  1. 01 Serving the Church

    We exist to serve the Church and to fulfil the Great Commission of making disciples of all nations.

  2. 02 Motivated by Love

    Everything we do is motivated by the love of Christ, reaching the world with compassion and grace.

  3. 03 Absolute Accountability

    We maintain absolute accountability in all areas of our ministry, finances, and relationships.

  4. 04 God's Kingdom First

    We prioritize God's Kingdom above all else, seeking His will in every decision and direction.

  5. 05 Raising Up Leaders

    We are committed to raising up national Christian leaders who will serve their own communities.

PHILOSOPHY

Our Approach

MVA partners with national leaders who already have strong relationships with churches and Christian organisations. The partnerships are cross-cultural, and the shape of the ministry is not the same in every place. The aim is to walk with local communities, rather than leaving them dependent on teams from outside.

Partnership-Focused

We work alongside local leaders, respecting their vision and authority in their communities.

Cross-Cultural

We celebrate and honor cultural diversity in our ministry approach and expressions.

Sustainable

We build relationships and capacity that will continue and flourish long after our involvement.

Be Part of Our Mission

Pray, give, or join a team. Each one helps equip leaders across the Asia-Pacific.
